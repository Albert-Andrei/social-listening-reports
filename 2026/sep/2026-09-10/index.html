Insert rate compares tonight’s gathered records against mentions the sentiment stage cleared in the same window, so it is a pipeline-lag measure, not a loss measure — and tonight it is close to meaningless as a percentage. At 518% overall it is even further outside the 39–83% band of Sep 1–8 than last night’s 353%, and the extreme per-platform readings (9805% on Reddit, 4625% on Web) are the arithmetic of a near-zero denominator rather than anything meaningful about those platforms. Read the two columns as different units: gathered counts billed API records including duplicates filtered before storage, while inserted counts mentions the sentiment stage wrote out, whenever they were collected. The backlog drain has now run two nights — an excess of 15,965 mentions over gathered tonight against 19,923 last night. That it shrank by 20% is the encouraging part: it is consistent with a large but finite queue being worked off, and the answer to last night’s open question is that inserted did not fall back toward the size of the haul, so the backlog is deeper than one night could clear. Keywords w/ new mentions = the platform’s sentiment-job count minus its {"analyzed":0} bucket; 305 of the 936 keyword-platform pairs produced something. API calls are the fixed per-keyword request counts, which is exactly why a collapse in yield does not produce a matching collapse in cost.
