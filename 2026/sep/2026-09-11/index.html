Insert rate compares tonight’s gathered records against mentions the sentiment stage cleared in the same window, so it is a pipeline-lag measure, not a loss measure — and for the third night it is close to meaningless as a percentage. At 282% overall it is still far outside the 39–83% band of Sep 1–8, and the extreme per-platform readings (9120% on Reddit, 5130% on Web) are the arithmetic of a near-zero denominator rather than anything meaningful about those platforms. Read the two columns as different units: gathered counts billed API records including duplicates filtered before storage, while inserted counts mentions the sentiment stage wrote out, whenever they were collected. The backlog drain has now run three nights and is close to finished — an excess of 4,347 mentions over gathered tonight, against 15,965 last night and 19,923 the night before. That is a 73% reduction in one night, and it is the expected shape of a large but finite queue being worked off; the falling insert total is therefore not a second fault.
