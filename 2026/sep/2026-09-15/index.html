🚨 First failed social listening jobs in weeks: three socialListeningSearchReddit jobs hit the 900-second workTimeout while waiting on Bright Data snapshots. The run was otherwise complete — 1,901 jobs spawned, 1,898 completed, 3 failed, 0 cancelled across 112 keywords — but this ends a six-night streak of zero failures. The failures are not random: all three were wide-window syncs, and none of the 94 brand keywords on 24-hour windows was affected. Two were topic-cron keywords pulling 7-day Reddit histories and the third was a brand-new keyword’s 30-day initial backfill; each was aborted at exactly 900s by throwIfAborted in brightdataProvider.ts while polling for a snapshot that was not ready. Bright Data was slow rather than broken, and one snapshot proves it: job WepxSyojKRxr8vF6e was failed at 04:16:11, then delivered 7 records at 04:18:21 — 17.2 minutes in, 2.2 minutes past its own deadline. Those records were billed and discarded. The blast radius is small and bounded: 3 of 112 keywords lost their Reddit slice for the night, and because a sentiment job only spawns behind a successful search, 3 analyzeMentions jobs were never created — which is exactly why analyzeMentions reads 893, not 896. Read the collection numbers carefully tonight, because the headline is misleading. Bright Data appears to have surged to 18.11 records per keyword from 14.33, comfortably above the 11.6–13.9 healthy band — but 44% of its records came from the 18 wide-window keywords, and tonight’s pool carried 14 topic keywords and 4 thirty-day backfills against 3 topic keywords last night. On the like-for-like 94-keyword 24-hour subset the vendor delivered 12.14 records per keyword, which is inside the healthy band but only at its lower edge, and the Web canary went backwards on that same subset: 4.82 records per keyword against 6.88. So the correct reading is that Bright Data is back in band on volume while remaining slow and uneven — LinkedIn nearly doubled, TikTok and Facebook fell, and Reddit’s snapshots are now slow enough to blow a 15-minute budget. Separately, the entitlement WARN storm has restarted after six days of silence: disabling social listening for workspaces logged 275 lines in the last 24 hours having been flat at zero since Sep 8, including 8 inside tonight’s window. Both crons ran on time, staging correctly processed nothing, and no keyword was left in a syncing state. Action: raise or chunk the Reddit workTimeout for multi-day windows — a 900s budget is now too tight for a 7-day Bright Data snapshot — and reopen the entitlement ticket.

Insert rate compares tonight’s gathered records against mentions the sentiment stage cleared in the same window, so it is a pipeline-lag measure, not a loss measure. At 153% overall it has widened again, from 102% last night, and the excess of inserted over gathered is 2,199 mentions against 58 last night — so a backlog that had been fully drained for two nights has started to refill. That is worth watching but it is far from the 282–518% of Sep 10–12: the sentiment stage is keeping up in absolute terms, it is simply clearing more than one night’s collection. Every platform sits between 135% and 246%, which is the signature of a uniform backlog flush rather than one platform misbehaving. Read the two columns as different units even so: gathered counts billed API records including duplicates filtered before storage, while inserted counts mentions the sentiment stage wrote out, whenever they were collected. TikTok’s 246% is the widest ratio and it is a small-denominator artefact — 145 records gathered, so residual insertions move the ratio easily. Keywords w/ new mentions = the platform’s sentiment-job count minus its {"analyzed":0} bucket; 281 of the 893 keyword-platform pairs produced something, against 237 of 776 last night. API calls are the fixed per-keyword request counts, which is why the invoice tracks coverage rather than yield in both directions — and note that Reddit was still charged 112 calls despite 3 of them timing out, because the snapshots were requested and, in at least one case, delivered.
