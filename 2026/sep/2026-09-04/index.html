But the pool collapsed from 225 keywords to 63. Most of that was predicted and correct: topic keywords went 89 → 5 purely because the 89 that ran last night are on a 7-day rule and are not due again until Sep 10. The brand side is the problem. Brand keywords re-sync every 24 hours, yet the cron picked up 58 where last night it picked up 136: 50 keywords returned, 8 were new arrivals taking a first-time 7-day backfill, and 86 brand keywords that synced last night simply did not appear. Of the 114 keywords restored in last night’s recovery, only 34 came back and 80 dropped straight back out, so roughly seven in ten of the recovery has already reverted in a single day. Staleness timing does not explain it: tonight’s cron accepted a keyword whose previous sync stamp was as recent as 04:00:12 — 23h30m old — and at most 23 of last night’s 227 search-job completions landed after that cutoff, so at least 63 of the 86 missing keywords were eligible on freshness grounds and were still not selected. The likeliest cause is the one still sitting unfixed in the backlog: the [customer.subscription.updated] disabling social listening for workspaces because new subscriptions do not have social listening enabled WARN has now fired 3,871 times across 2,625 distinct workspaces since Aug 19, up 299 lines and 182 newly affected workspaces in the last 24 hours — its largest single-day step so far. Everything downstream simply reflects the smaller pool: 4,200 records gathered, 2,536 mentions inserted and $8.51 of spend against $36.89 last night. That fall is arithmetic, not a second fault. One new item to watch: the Bright Data Reddit invalid-schema warning went from 2 skipped records to 14.
