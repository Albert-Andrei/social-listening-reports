The new fault: six socialListeningSearchYoutube jobs died in under a second on an HTTP 403 from the Data API v3 videos endpoint, reason forbidden on the myRating parameter, raised inside getVideoDetails while enriching already-discovered videos. These are not timeouts and not quota exhaustion — the search itself succeeded and the job then threw while fetching statistics, so the request is being rejected as unauthorised for a parameter it should not need. It is narrow but real: 6 of 168 keywords lost their YouTube night entirely, and because a sentiment job only spawns behind a clean search, those keywords were never analysed. The 900-second workTimeout bug is now in its fourth consecutive night — 3 socialListeningSearchTiktok jobs breached it (900.2s, 900.7s and 901.1s), one on a 24-hour brand window and two on 7-day topic windows, and a Twitter job failed the same way at only 210s, which means X search runs a much tighter budget than the Bright Data jobs. Three of those four late-completed and their records — 2, 9 and 14 — were billed and then discarded unanalysed. Bright Data recovered broadly tonight, and that is the good news. On the like-for-like 147-keyword brand subset the vendor delivered 9.58 records per keyword against 6.22 last night, and the move has the breadth that makes it credible: five of its six platforms rose — Facebook 0.39 → 1.05, Instagram 0.50 → 1.66, TikTok 0.32 → 0.40, LinkedIn 0.40 → 0.71 and the Web canary 3.56 → 5.14 — with only Reddit falling (1.04 → 0.63). Crucially, the two non-Bright-Data vendors moved the other way — X 9.03 → 8.11 records per keyword and YouTube 6.96 → 6.09 on the keywords that ran, both softer by about a tenth — so tonight was not a generally richer night for social content. That divergence is what localises the movement to one vendor. The entitlement WARN storm is in its fourth night and grew: disabling social listening for workspaces logged 299 lines over 236 companies in the last 24 hours against 277 over 214, with 16 firings inside tonight’s window and a cumulative 6,049 lines since Aug 19. Action: the YouTube getVideoDetails call is requesting an authorisation scope it does not have and needs fixing before it spreads — and the workTimeout ticket has now been open four nights and has spread from Bright Data to X.
