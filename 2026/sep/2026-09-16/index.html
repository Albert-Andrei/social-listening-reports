🚨 A DNS resolution failure on the production job pods, not a vendor outage, is what broke tonight’s run. 2,067 jobs spawned, 2,061 completed, 8 failed, 0 cancelled across 122 keywords — the most failures in a single night in this series, against 3 last night and 0 for the six nights before that. Six of the eight failures land in a 70-second burst between 03:44:54 and 03:46:04, and they name two different vendors. Five were getaddrinfo errors against api.brightdata.com (three EAI_AGAIN, two ENOTFOUND) across four platforms — Web, Instagram and two TikTok jobs plus one Reddit job — and the sixth was a socialListeningComputeMetrics job that failed on getaddrinfo EAI_AGAIN api.x.com, surfaced as No response from API. That a Bright Data hostname and an X hostname failed to resolve within the same minute is what rules out a vendor fault and points at the pod’s resolver: the window holds 105 getaddrinfo lines in total, 80 in app:web and 25 in app:job, so social listening was a bystander to something platform-wide, and five of the six failures came from a single job pod. The stack traces put the failures inside awaitBrightDataKeywordSnapshot under a retryPromise loop, which is why their durations scatter from 176ms to 528s rather than clustering. The remaining two failures are last night’s bug, unchanged: one Reddit and one TikTok job, both 7-day topic-cron windows, hit the 900-second workTimeout — and both then finished anyway. The Reddit one delivered 98 records 42 seconds after it had already been marked failed, and the TikTok one delivered 5 records 59 seconds after; because a sentiment job only spawns behind a clean search, all 103 records were billed and thrown away. That is also why analyzeMentions reads 969 rather than 976: 7 sentiment jobs were never created, and it is the whole explanation for Reddit’s 79% insert rate. Underneath the incident, Bright Data had its best night since the collapse — but read it on the like-for-like subset, not the headline. The vendor appears to have jumped to 29.79 records per keyword from 18.11, yet 55% of its records came from the 29 keywords running 7-day windows, against 18 wide-window keywords last night. On the 94 brand keywords, all on identical 24-hour windows, Bright Data delivered 17.22 records per keyword against 12.14 last night, and the Web canary reached 8.96 against 4.82. That is the canary’s best reading since the collapse began on Sep 10, though still below its 11.6–13.9 healthy band, and the move is broad rather than narrow: four of the six Bright Data platforms rose on the brand subset, with only LinkedIn and Reddit falling. The entitlement WARN storm is now in its second night: disabling social listening for workspaces logged 282 lines over 227 companies in the last 24 hours, against 275 last night and zero for the six days before, with 16 firings inside tonight’s window and a cumulative 5,468 lines over 3,607 distinct companies since Aug 19. Both crons ran on time, staging correctly processed nothing, no keyword was left in a syncing state, and Reddit logged zero invalid-schema skips on 716 records. Action: this is an infrastructure ticket first — investigate DNS resolution on the job pods, since the same minute broke two unrelated vendors and 105 lookups app-wide. Then chunk the Bright Data workTimeout for multi-day windows, and keep the entitlement ticket open.

Mentions: gathered vs inserted per platform

Gathered = exact sum of result.count over the 971 Mezmo search-job completions; inserted = exact sum of analyzed over the 969 sentiment-job completions. The per-platform job counts carry the incident in plain sight: Facebook, YouTube, Twitter/X and LinkedIn returned all 122, Instagram, Reddit and Web returned 121 and TikTok 120 — and both distributions reconcile against their unfiltered aggregates (6,704 gathered, 6,568 inserted, 593 and 595 zero buckets, 62 and 63 distinct values), including the full non-zero bucket multiset, which is what rules out a mis-scoped platform filter that matching totals alone could hide. Tonight the insert rate falls below 100% for the first time in this series, and that is meaningful rather than noise. At 98% overall the pipeline analysed 136 fewer mentions than it collected, against a 153% backlog flush last night, and the shortfall is almost entirely Reddit at 79% — 716 records gathered but 569 analysed, because the 98 records that arrived after their job had been failed never got a sentiment job. Six platforms sit at 100–101%, which is what a drained queue looks like: collection and analysis matching one-for-one. Read the two columns as different units even so: gathered counts billed API records including duplicates filtered before storage, while inserted counts mentions the sentiment stage wrote out, whenever they were collected. Breadth improved sharply: 374 of the 969 keyword-platform pairs produced something, against 281 of 893 last night, and Web led on 77 keywords. The per-keyword figures in this table are inflated by window width and should not be compared with earlier nights — 52% of all records came from the 29 keywords running 7-day windows, so the brand-subset numbers in the notes column are the comparable ones. API calls are the fixed per-keyword request counts, which is why the invoice tracks coverage rather than yield, and note that every failed job was still charged its call: Reddit billed 122 calls even though one job resolved no DNS and another blew its deadline.
