Mentions: gathered vs inserted per platform

Gathered counts the records each Bright Data, X or YouTube call actually billed for, read from the exact result.count distribution of every completed search job. Inserted counts the mentions the sentiment stage wrote out, read from the analyzed distribution of the matching analyzeMentions jobs. The gap between them is overwhelmingly duplicate filtering, not loss: a record already stored from an earlier night is re-collected, re-billed and then discarded. Tonight the two totals sit 637 records apart, 97% overall, and that gap is unusually concentrated — Reddit alone accounts for 247 of it and TikTok for a further 118, leaving the other six platforms within 0–4% of parity. The gathered total is the highest this series has recorded and the inserted total the second highest, which is what a 260-keyword cohort on a recovering yield night looks like.
